Motion should explain what changed. Keep it short and let it get out of the way.

<MotionTokens />

## Guidelines

- Most interface transitions take 100 to 200ms.
- Use decelerate curves for things entering and accelerate curves for things leaving.
- Respect reduced motion. Wrap anything larger than a fade in `motion-safe:`.
