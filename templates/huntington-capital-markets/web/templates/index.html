Methodology & Strategic Rationale

How these tools work, why they matter, and how they create compounding value for Huntington Capital Markets.

Strategic Impact

These tools don't replace bankers. They make every banker operate like they have a team of three behind them.

Scale Coverage

Cover more sponsors at the same depth without proportional headcount growth. The 40% revenue growth doesn't require 40% more analysts.

Originate Proactively

Shift from reactive RFP responses to proactive idea generation. Every sponsor call becomes a deal origination opportunity, not just relationship maintenance.

Move Up Tables

The path from regional peer to PNC-class franchise is about density of coverage and quality of ideas. AI delivers both at a fraction of the traditional cost base.

The Problem We're Solving

Huntington's Capital Markets fee revenue grew 40% in two years — from $248M to $346M. Headcount didn't grow 40%. The result is a capacity gap: bankers are stretched across more relationships, more products, and more complex deal pipelines than ever before.

1. Opportunity Scanner

What It Does

Continuously monitors sponsor portfolios for transaction signals — fund lifecycle events, maturity walls, portfolio activity, exit signals, and dividend recap windows — and proactively surfaces actionable opportunities for the coverage team.

Why It Matters

PE firms are sitting on $4 trillion in unrealized portfolio value. Every one of those assets is a refinancing, a recap, or an exit waiting to happen. The difference between being in the room when those conversations start versus reacting to an RFP is the difference between lead left arranger and participant. The Scanner ensures Huntington shows up with the idea first.

How It Works

  • Fund Lifecycle Signals: Tracks fund vintage, deployment percentage, and DPI to identify sponsors actively deploying or seeking exits
  • Maturity Wall Analysis: Monitors debt maturity schedules across sponsor portfolios to flag refinancing windows 12-18 months before maturity
  • Portfolio Activity: Detects bolt-on acquisition pipelines, operational improvements, and growth capital needs
  • Exit Signals: Identifies restructuring completion, IPO preparation, and sponsor-to-sponsor secondary timing

Each opportunity includes the recommended Huntington product, estimated fee, confidence score, and a specific next action for the coverage banker.

2. Sponsor Briefing Pack

What It Does

Generates a complete 2-3 page briefing pack for any PE sponsor in 2 minutes using a multi-turn AI conversation. Three sequential passes produce increasingly specific output: executive summary, portfolio analysis with deal-level financing recommendations, and MD call talking points.

Why It Matters

Every sponsor coverage call starts with prep. Today, an analyst spends 2-4 hours pulling CRM notes, scanning deal databases, and building a briefing. That's 2-4 hours of analyst time per call, across dozens of sponsor relationships. This tool compresses that to minutes while producing output that's more specific and more current than manual prep.

How It Works

  • Turn 1: AI ingests sponsor profile (fund, partners, themes, portfolio, deal activity, relationship history) and generates an executive summary focused on what matters for this call
  • Turn 2: AI layers in current market conditions (leveraged loan pricing, sector spreads, CLO formation) and generates per-company financing recommendations with deal sizing and fee estimates, plus 3 proactive pitch ideas
  • Turn 3: AI produces call talking points — opener, discussion topics with specific questions, and a concrete follow-up commitment
  • Follow-up Q&A: The MD can ask ad-hoc questions and get instant answers grounded in the briefing context

3. PE Bolt-On Scout

What It Does

Systematically matches target acquisitions against PE sponsor criteria to identify bolt-on introduction opportunities. Produces ranked match lists with banker-ready rationales for each introduction.

Why It Matters

Chris Wood said it best: "We have so many founder-owned businesses that would be nice bolt-ons to some of the portfolio companies that GTCR, Stone Point, CD&R, and Madison Dearborn own." Today, that matching logic lives in individual bankers' heads. It's inconsistent, incomplete, and doesn't scale. This tool makes it systematic — ensuring every viable introduction gets surfaced, not just the ones a particular banker happens to think of.

How It Works

  • Turn 1 (Scoring): AI evaluates every company in Huntington's client book against the sponsor's stated criteria — sector fit, size fit, geography, ownership readiness, and strategic alignment with existing platforms. Uses Claude Sonnet for throughput.
  • Turn 2 (Rationale): For the top 5 matches, AI generates detailed, banker-ready rationales that reference specific market conditions, transaction signals, and strategic fit. Uses Claude Sonnet for quality.
  • Turn 3 (Formatting): AI formats the output as a polished "Top 5 Introductions This Quarter" memo ready for a sponsor meeting, including talking points for each introduction.

The Compounding Effect

Every introduction that converts to a deal creates two fee streams: sell-side advisory for the Huntington client, and acquisition financing for the sponsor. The tool turns coverage relationships into deal origination at scale.

4. Leadership View

What It Does

Aggregates the full sponsor coverage pipeline into an executive dashboard showing pipeline composition by product line, sector concentration, sponsor activity levels, and deal stage progression. Designed as the Monday morning view for Capital Markets leadership.

Why It Matters

As Huntington scales its Capital Markets franchise, leadership needs visibility into where the pipeline is concentrated, which relationships are generating activity, and where the coverage gaps are. Today, this requires pulling data from multiple systems and manually assembling a view. The Leadership Dashboard does it automatically and highlights the decisions that need attention.

How It Works

  • Product Line Mix: Shows fee pipeline distribution across LevFin, M&A Advisory, IG DCM, and ECM — with concentration warnings when any line exceeds 70%
  • Sector Heatmap: Reveals where Huntington is overweight or underweight relative to market opportunity
  • Sponsor Activity: Tracks deal flow by sponsor to identify which relationships are producing and which have gone quiet
  • Stage Pipeline: Shows probability-weighted fee projections by deal stage (Active, Pitched, Pipeline, Monitoring)

Decisions It Enables

Where should the next hire sit? Which sector needs more coverage? Which sponsor relationship needs a call this week? Which product line needs diversification? The dashboard turns these from quarterly review topics into weekly action items.

  • Authentication: AWS IAM — no API keys, role-based access control
